# Account, Plans and Devices

WireFrame's design tools are free with no time limit. An account ties your copy of the app to a plan — which decides the AI Copilot's credit and how many computers you can use at once — and opens the full posts on the blog.

## Sign in

The first time the app starts it asks you to sign in. Choose **Sign in with browser**: your browser opens wireframe.com.vn, you sign in with **email**, **Google** or **GitHub**, and the app is signed in when you come back — no password is typed into the app. A new browser gets a 6-digit code by email the first time.

The account menu (top right of the window) shows who is signed in and the plan, and has:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Manage account, plan & devices** | Opens your account page on the website |
| **Sign out of this device** | Signs out and frees this computer's device slot |
| **Check for Updates** | Asks for a newer release now |
| **Star repository**, **Donate / Buy me a coffee** | Ways to support an independent EDA tool |

## Devices

Each plan allows a number of computers signed in at the same time. To use another one, sign one out — from that computer, or from **Manage account, plan & devices** on the website, which lists every device with its last use.

## The trial, and what comes after

- **Free-Trial** starts the first time you sign in **from the desktop app**: every tool, plus a little WireFrame credit for the AI Copilot. One trial per computer.
- After it, the plan becomes **Free**: every design tool stays, with no time limit, and the Copilot keeps working on your own model key (see [Models, Credit and Your Data](../ai/models-and-credits.md)).
- **Plus** and **Pro** add a monthly AI credit and more devices; **Enterprise** is by agreement.

Prices and amounts are on the [pricing page](https://wireframe.com.vn/pricing). A plan's end date is counted on WireFrame's clock, not your computer's.

## Working offline

After signing in, the app works without a network. It confirms your plan with WireFrame at least every **14 days**; within that time nothing needs to be online except the AI Copilot (which needs its model provider) and online library downloads.

## Usage statistics

WireFrame can send usage statistics — feature names, counts, session length, the version and the operating system — to help decide what to build next. They never contain project contents, file names, part numbers or AI prompts. Turn them off in **Preferences → Updates** (**Usage statistics**), or from the account page on the website.

## Updates

**Check for Updates** in the account menu, or **Preferences → Updates → Check now**, looks for a newer release; **Help → Download Latest Version** opens the download page. Installers for Windows, macOS on Apple Silicon and Linux are on [wireframe.com.vn/download](https://wireframe.com.vn/download) and on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/CaSauCoin/WireFrame_Product/releases).

## Report a problem or ask for a feature

**Help → Report a Bug...** and **Help → Request a Feature...** open a form inside the app that sends your report with the app's version and your operating system. You can also open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/CaSauCoin/WireFrame_Product/issues). Leave out private project files, keys and customer data.
